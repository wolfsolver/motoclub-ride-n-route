B2B2C Multi-Tenant Moto-Tourism Platform

Bridging Club Logistics & the Riding Experience

Built for Motorcycle Clubs, Ambassadors, and Riders.

MotoClub Ride&Route solves the logistical challenges of motorcycle club organizers, empowers Moto-tourism Ambassadors to publish and protect curated GPX routes, and gives every rider a single hub for club memberships, digital garages, itineraries, and events.

Born from the real-world needs of Riders Club Ryu (Royal Enfield Milano Nord), eliminating the chaos of scattered WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets, and unprotected GPX files.

Built for the Entire Mototourism Ecosystem

One progressive web application tailored for clubs, content creators, and everyday motorcyclists.

🛡️

For Clubs

Complete roster & membership fee management, annual renewals, digital QR membership cards, event planning with automated waitlists, restaurant/hotel headcounts, merch reservations, pre-ride check-ins, and audience building via Follower & Public Teaser modes.

💎

For Ambassadors

Verified VIP showcases for motorcycle tourism professionals to publish curated itineraries, group Protected GPX routes into Premium Bundles, track unique rider downloads, and grant bundle access directly to their followers in a privacy-first workflow.

🏍️

For Riders

Personal digital Garage, multi-club memberships or lightweight club following, and access to a community-rated repository of GPX itineraries classified by difficulty (Rookie, Medium, Advanced, Off-road) with interactive elevation profiles and maps.

Platform Architecture & Ecosystem Overview

How MotoClub Ride&Route connects Clubs, Ambassadors, and Riders in a unified experience.

Core Capabilities & Technology

Engineered as an offline-capable Progressive Web App with strict database-level RBAC security.

1. Multi-Tenant Club Environments

Independent spaces for clubs with custom branding, public/private visibility, mono-brand restriction support, and opt-in public teasers.

2. Member & QR Verification

Manage Followers, active Members, and Coordinators with ephemeral 30-second QR codes for live membership and event check-in verification.

3. Granular Event Logistics

Day trips and multi-day tours with capacity limits, automatic waitlist promotion, and RSVP options for pillion riders, lunch, and overnight stays.

4. Protected GPX & Premium Bundles

Token-protected GPX downloads (`Private`, `Club`, `Public`, and `Premium` scopes), Ambassador bundle management, and community reviews.

5. Club Merchandising

Reserve-now, pickup-later catalog for club apparel and gadgets, integrated with event check-ins for seamless distribution.

6. Verified RBAC Security

Nearly 400 automated Cucumber BDD security scenarios validating PocketBase collection rules, privacy isolation, and role permissions.
