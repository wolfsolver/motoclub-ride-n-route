1. Overview & Platform Roles

MotoClubApp brings Motorcycle Club logistics, group ride planning, curated GPX route sharing, club merchandise, and digital membership cards together in a single Progressive Web App (PWA). Available features depend on your role in the active context:

Role Description Capabilities
Guest Unauthenticated visitor Read-only access to public content (public clubs, public events, public routes, Ambassador showcases, teasers) and QR card verification (/verify/:tokenId).
Registered Rider Verified account user Manages Profile and Digital Garage, publishes public/private routes, joins public events, follows Clubs (Follower) and Ambassadors, requests club membership or new club creation.
Follower User following a Club Receives club updates and sees teaser previews of members-only events without accessing internal details or restricted RSVPs.
Member Official Club member Switches into Club Mode, accesses Members_Only events, club routes, Digital Membership Card with QR verification, and the Club Merchandise store.
Coordinator Club Administrator Approves/imports members, manages renewals, creates official events, runs meetup check-ins, manages merch orders, sends club broadcasts, and configures club settings.
Ambassador Verified mototourism creator Publishes Premium routes, manages Bundles, and grants followers permanent access to protected GPX tracks.
SuperAdmin Global platform admin Operates the /superadmin console: activates/suspends Clubs, approves Ambassadors, and governs the global motorcycle brands vocabulary.
Multi-Club Note: Member and Coordinator roles are club-scoped. You can be a Coordinator in Club A and a Member in Club B, switching seamlessly between them.

2. Interface & Navigation Structure

The app interface (/app) is organized into three main areas:

Top Bar

  • Left Button (Operating Context): opens the left drawer to switch context (Free Rider or one of your Clubs).
  • Center Title: shows the active context (Free Rider, Guest, or the selected Club name with its custom theme color).
  • Right Button (Personal Account): opens the right drawer. Displays a red numeric badge for unread notifications and an amber dot when your profile or push notifications need attention.

Bottom Navigation (4 Core Tabs)

  1. Home: personalized overview, profile/push completeness alerts, upcoming events, featured routes, and Most Followed Club/Ambassador leaderboards.
  2. Routes: GPX tracks and routes repository (Club, Public, Private, Mine).
  3. Events: calendar of rides and rallies (Club, Public, Registered, Mine).
  4. Clubs: directory and search for active Motorcycle Clubs, membership requests, and new club creation.

Left Drawer (Operating Context)

  • Switch Context: choose Use as Free Rider or any club in My Clubs.
  • Manage / Other Clubs: opens the club discovery and management view.
  • Active Club Section (Club Mode only): My Membership Card and Merchandise.
  • Club Coordination (Coordinators in Club Mode only): Member Requests & Approvals, Merchandise Orders, Club Profile Settings, Broadcast to Members.
  • Ambassador Community (visible to all): Ambassador directory and, for approved Ambassadors, Bundles & Followers Panel.

Right Drawer (Personal Account)

  • Rider Profile (with To complete badge when applicable)
  • Notifications (with unread counter)
  • Digital Garage
  • My Merch Orders (cross-club personal order history)
  • Language (English / Italiano), Sign Out, and Build Info at the bottom.

3. Installing as an App (PWA)

MotoClubApp is a Progressive Web App: you can install it directly on your phone or computer without an app store. Installing is strongly recommended and required on iPhone/iPad to receive Web Push Notifications.

  • iPhone / iPad (Safari, iOS 16.4+): open MotoClubApp in Safari → tap Share → select Add to Home Screen → close Safari and launch the app from the new Home Screen icon.
  • Android (Chrome / Edge): open the browser menu (⋮) → tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  • Desktop (Chrome / Edge): click the install icon in the address bar to run MotoClubApp as a standalone desktop app.

4. Sign In, Registration & Account Security

Guest Mode

When opening the app, you can close the welcome modal to browse as a read-only Guest, or sign in right away.

Registration & Email Verification

  1. Open Sign In / Register and switch to the sign-up tab.
  2. Enter your Email and Password (with confirmation).
  3. Open the verification email and click the link to activate your account.

Sign In & Password Reset

  • Use the eye icon inside the password field to show or hide your password while typing.
  • If you forgot your password (or were pre-provisioned directly by a Club and need to set your initial password), use Forgot Password to receive a reset link via email.

5. Personal Account: Profile, Push & Garage

Open Right Drawer → Rider Profile (or tap the completeness banner on Home).

5.1 Public Profile & Showcase

  • Display Name & Avatar: shown in event rosters, routes, and community reviews.
  • Public Showcase: Bio, Social Link, Website, and Operating Province (used for approximate distance calculations when GPS is off).
  • Ambassador Application: apply for verified Ambassador status to publish Premium routes and Bundles (reviewed by SuperAdmin).

5.2 Private Safety Details (PII)

Strictly private: visible only to you and, for membership & ride safety purposes, Coordinators of your active clubs.

  • Mandatory Club Identity Fields: First Name, Last Name, Tax Code, Birth Date, and Birth Place.
  • Safety & Logistics: Phone, Experience Level (Rookie, Rider, Master, Wild), Emergency Contact (ICE), Food Intolerances / Allergies.
  • Preferences: Default Club at startup and Home Location.
Important: You must complete your private identity details before requesting to join a Club. Once you are an active or pending member, civil identity fields are locked to preserve registry integrity.

5.3 Completeness Indicators (To complete)

If your Public Profile, Private Details, or Web Push Notifications are incomplete or inactive, the app displays the amber dot in the TopBar, the To complete badge in the Right Drawer, and quick-jump shortcuts on Home.

5.4 Web Push Notifications & Notification Center

In the Web Push Notifications section of your Profile, tap Enable Push Notifications and choose which categories trigger device push alerts (Urgent Club Broadcasts, Normal Club Broadcasts, Personal Messages, Event Reminders, Membership & Card Status, Merchandise In Stock).

All notifications always remain available in the Notification Center (Right Drawer → Notifications), with All / Unread filtering, deep-link opening, and automatic expiration.

5.5 Digital Garage

In Right Drawer → Digital Garage, add, edit, or remove your motorcycles (official Brand or Other, Model, Year). You can pick a bike from your Garage when RSVPing to events (required for Brand_Only events).

6. Home, Geolocation & Maps Navigation

What's on Home

  • Completeness Status: quick checklist for Public Profile, Private Details, and Push Notifications.
  • Upcoming Events & Featured Routes: curated preview for your active context.
  • Community Leaderboards: Most Followed Clubs, Most Followed Ambassadors, and Ambassador of the Month.

GPS Location & Approximate Distance

To respect privacy and iOS/Safari policies, the app does not trigger automatic background GPS prompts: tap Use my location (or Refresh GPS) whenever you want to use live GPS. Otherwise, distances fall back to your Operating Province, Home Location, or the Club headquarters (for Follower teasers). If blocked (Location blocked), re-enable location access in your browser or iOS settings.

Opening Navigation (Open in Maps)

  • iPhone / iPad (iOS/iPadOS): opens Apple Maps (maps.apple.com).
  • Android: opens the native maps app chooser (geo:).
  • Desktop: opens Google Maps in the browser.

7. Routes & GPX Repository

In the Routes tab you can browse Club, Public, Private, and Mine tabs.

Difficulty Levels

  • Rookie (Touristic): relaxed routes under 150–200 km.
  • Rider (Dynamic): medium distances (200–350 km) with twisty roads and fluid pace.
  • Master (Iron Butt): over 350–400 km, tight hairpins, and long hours in the saddle.
  • Wild (Off-Road): dirt tracks, gravel, and dust for Enduro and Scrambler riders.

Creating & Detail Actions

  • Create Route (+): set title, summary, Markdown description, starting point, distance, difficulty, scope (Private, Club, Public, or Premium for Ambassadors), .gpx file, photo/map cover, and optional external website link.
  • Download GPX: token-protected .gpx download for OsmAnd+, Garmin, or TomTom (requires sign-in).
  • Open Original Website: opens the external source website linked to the route (when provided).
  • Use for my Club (Forking): Coordinators can clone a public route into their Club while preserving author attribution.
  • Create Event from Route: immediately create a new event linked to the track.
  • Reviews & Sharing: rate with stars, perceived difficulty, and comments; share direct links.

8. Events, RSVPs & Meetup Check-In

Event Visibility Tiers

  • Public: visible to everyone (including Guests and Free Riders).
  • Members Only (Members_Only): full details for active club members; Followers see a Teaser (title, date, distance from Club HQ).
  • Brand Only (Brand_Only): requires selecting a matching brand motorcycle from your Garage.
  • Coordinators Only (Coordinators_Only): internal organizational events visible only to Club Coordinators.

Joining an Event (RSVP)

  1. Open the event and tap Join / Manage RSVP.
  2. Select Confirmed, Maybe, or Withdrawn.
  3. Choose your Garage bike, passenger, lunch, overnight options, and notes.
  4. Difficulty Warning: if the event difficulty exceeds your profile experience level, a safety warning is shown before confirming.
  5. Automatic Waitlist: when capacity is reached, new RSVPs enter the waitlist and are automatically promoted in FIFO order if a confirmed rider withdraws.
  6. Add to Calendar: export the event to your device calendar.

Meetup Check-In (Coordinators)

When an event is Open, Coordinators see the live Logistics Summary (bikes, passengers, lunches, overnights, check-ins), filter attendees (Confirmed, Missing Check-in, Checked In), mark arrivals with Check-in, use Quick Call (rider or ICE contact) for latecomers, and hand over club merchandise orders on-site.

9. Clubs, Context Switching & Digital Card

Discovering, Following, or Joining

  • Follow Club (Follower): when enabled by the club, follow with one tap to receive updates and see members-only event teasers.
  • Request to Join (Member): requires completed private identity details; stays Pending until approved by a Coordinator (cancellable anytime).
  • Request New Club: from the Clubs tab, tap Request New Club to submit a new Motorcycle Club (requires completed Private Safety Details civil identity fields in your Rider Profile).
    • Club Name (required): official name of the Motorcycle Club.
    • Subtitle / Motto (optional): short tagline or local chapter name.
    • Physical Headquarters / Club Address (optional, recommended): address or city with geographic autocomplete (coordinates for distance and maps).
    • Club Manifesto, Bio & Rules (optional): description of club activities, spirit, and rules.
    • Reference Brand Color (optional, default #eab308) and Club Emblem / Logo (optional, image file).
    • Visibility toggles: Club visible in public directory (enabled by default) and Allow non-member riders to Follow this club (Follower mode & Teasers).
    • Activation: you are registered as a Coordinator awaiting SuperAdmin approval; once active, you can also configure website URL, annual fee, and renewal grace period.
  • Share Club: direct permalink (/app/clubs/:clubId) and scannable Club QR Code.

Free Rider Mode vs Club Mode

From the Left Drawer (Operating Context), switch between Use as Free Rider and any of your active Clubs to apply the club's theme and unlock internal events, routes, membership card, and merchandise.

My Membership Card

In Club Mode, open Left Drawer → My Membership Card to view your member number, role title, validity year, and effective status (Active, In_Grace, Expired, Pending, Suspended). Active and In-Grace members get a 60-minute temporary QR Code (regenerable) verifiable without login at /verify/:tokenId.

10. Club Coordinator Tools

Available in Left Drawer → Club Coordination while in Club Mode:

  • Member Requests & Approvals: approve/reject requests, assign member numbers and role titles, record annual renewals, inspect member safety/ICE details, filter unverified accounts and resend invite, add single members or run bulk import (.xlsx, .csv, or paste with live validation), and export the roster to .csv.
  • Club Profile Settings: configure name, logo, theme color, HQ address, province, website, annual fee, renewal grace cutoff, and Follower mode.
  • Broadcast to Members: send Normal or Urgent notifications to all Active and In-Grace official members (Followers excluded), with optional event deep link and automatic expiration (7/30 days or custom).

11. Club Merchandise

  • For Members (Left Drawer → Merchandise): browse the club catalog, pick size/variant and custom fields, and reserve items without online payment.
  • Personal History (Right Drawer → My Merch Orders): track all your orders across clubs.
  • For Coordinators (Left Drawer → Merchandise Orders): manage catalog items, update order statuses (including bulk updates), export to .xlsx, and mark items delivered during event check-ins.

12. Ambassadors, Bundles & Premium Routes

  • Ambassador Directory (Left Drawer → Ambassador): browse verified Ambassadors and see the Ambassador of the Month.
  • Ambassador Showcase: bio, social/website links, Follower/Supporter counters, Follow button, public routes, and previews of Premium Routes and Bundles.
  • Premium Routes: display a public teaser; full GPX download and technical details unlock for authorized riders (who earn the Supporter badge on reviews).
  • Bundles & Followers Panel: for approved Ambassadors to create curated Bundles and grant permanent access to their Followers.

13. SuperAdmin Console (/superadmin)

Platform administrators sign in separately at /superadmin to view global telemetry, activate or suspend Clubs, approve Ambassador applications (including the Ambassador of the Month flag), and maintain the global motorcycle brands vocabulary.
